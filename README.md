# Velto — AI Task Planner

Velto is an AI-powered task planner designed to help people turn their goals and ideas into clear, realistic, actionable tasks.

Instead of spending time figuring out **what to do next**, users simply tell Velto what they want to accomplish, and Velto helps turn that goal into an organized plan they can customize and work through.

## ✨ What is Velto?

Velto is built for people who have goals but don't always know how to break them down into manageable steps.

Users can:

- 🎯 Describe a goal or what they need to accomplish
- 🤖 Generate an organized task plan with AI
- ✏️ Customize generated tasks
- ✅ Mark tasks as completed
- 📋 Manage their daily and long-term tasks
- 👤 Choose a planning style based on their role

### Planning Roles

Velto is designed for different types of users:

- 🎓 **Student** — studying, assignments, exams, projects
- 💼 **Professional** — work tasks and career goals
- 🚀 **Entrepreneur** — business ideas and projects
- 🌱 **Life & Goals** — personal goals, habits, and life planning
